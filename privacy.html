PRIVACY & SECURITY

Privacy and security

This documentation website is an installation and information guide. It does not ask students to enter their Samvidha password here.

Where should I log in?

Students should authenticate directly on the official Samvidha portal. Do not type your Samvidha password into this documentation website or send it to another person.

Open official Samvidha →

What this website is for

The site explains the extension, installation steps, attendance calculations and browser-specific usage. It is not a replacement for the official IARE portal.

Is this official IARE software?

No. The Attendance Planner is a student-built browser extension. The site clearly identifies that status so students can distinguish it from official IARE systems.

Safe installation

Use the published browser-store listing, review the extension permissions shown by your browser, and keep your browser updated. Avoid unofficial APKs, copied extension files or people requesting your Samvidha credentials.
