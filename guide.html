IARE ATTENDANCE CALCULATOR

How to calculate attendance percentage

The basic attendance percentage calculation is simple: divide the number of classes attended by the total number of classes held or conducted, then multiply by 100.

%

Attendance percentage

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Classes Held) × 100

For example, if a student attended 82 classes out of 100 held classes, the attendance percentage is 82%.

+

Classes needed to improve

If your attendance is below a selected target, you need to attend additional classes without missing them until the attendance percentage reaches the target.

−

Classes you can miss

The maximum number of future classes you can miss depends on your current attended classes, total held classes and the target percentage you want to maintain.

▦

Subject-wise attendance

Overall attendance can hide differences between subjects. Checking each subject separately helps identify subjects where attendance needs more attention.

ATTENDANCE TARGETS

75%, 80% and 85% attendance targets

Students may want to compare their current attendance with different target levels. The planner uses target calculations to make this easier to understand.

75

75% attendance

Use 75% as a target when you want to understand how close your current attendance is to that percentage.

The number of additional classes required or classes that can be missed depends on your current attendance record.

80

80% attendance

An 80% target provides a higher attendance buffer than a 75% target. Your exact required or missable classes depend on your current numbers.

85

85% attendance

An 85% target gives students a stricter attendance goal. It can be useful when planning future attendance and avoiding unnecessary absences.

FAQ

IARE attendance questions

How do I calculate IARE attendance percentage?

Attendance percentage is calculated as: classes attended divided by total classes held, multiplied by 100.

What is an IARE attendance calculator?

An IARE attendance calculator helps students understand their current attendance percentage and estimate future attendance requirements or possible absences based on a selected target.

Can I check subject-wise attendance?

Yes. Subject-wise attendance is useful because overall attendance can hide individual subjects where attendance is lower or higher.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 75%?

The answer depends on your current attended and total classes. The required number is the smallest number of future attended classes that makes the attendance percentage reach 75%.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 80%?

It depends on your current attendance record. The planner can help you understand how many additional attended classes are required to reach the selected target.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 85%?

The required number depends on your current attended and total classes. The higher the target, the more future attendance may be required.

How many classes can I miss?

The maximum number of classes you can miss depends on your current attendance and the target you want to maintain. The planner provides a can-bunk calculation for this purpose.

Is this the official IARE Student App?

No. IARE Attendance Planner is a student-built project. It is not the official IARE Student App, MyIARE application or official Samvidha service.

Do I need to give my Samvidha password to the planner?

No. Never give your Samvidha password to this website or another person. Authenticate directly through the official Samvidha portal.

Does the planner change official attendance?

No. The planner is intended to help students view and understand attendance-related information. It does not provide a mechanism to edit or fabricate official attendance.
