ATTENDANCE GUIDE

IARE Samvidha attendance planning explained

The planner is built around common IARE student questions: What is my attendance? Which subject is risky? How many classes can I miss? What do I need to reach 80% or 85%? What class is happening now?

Overall attendance

The overall attendance view gives a quick picture of attendance status without requiring you to manually interpret multiple figures.

Subject-wise attendance

Subject-level information helps identify subjects where missing another class could have a larger effect. Use the subject view before making attendance decisions.

75% → 80% → 85% target ladder

The target ladder shows attendance against common thresholds. Instead of only showing a single percentage, the planner helps you understand the distance to each target.

Can I bunk?

The planner can calculate how many valid future classes can potentially be missed while staying above a selected target. Treat this as a planning calculation, not a guarantee: timetable changes, attendance corrections and institutional rules can affect the final result.

NOW and next class

The planner uses the configured IARE timetable periods to identify the current class, remaining periods for the day and the next timetable day.

ImportantThe Attendance Planner does not give students a way to edit or fabricate official attendance. It is intended to help students understand and plan around information available through their normal Samvidha session.
